03 / The Playbook

Phase-by-Phase Breakdown

Every action item, in order, with nothing left to guesswork. Check these off as you go.

Phase 1
Foundation
March 30 - April 5 (This Week)
This is the most important week. Everything that happens from here depends on what you set up now. The song is done. Now you build the infrastructure around it.
  • Lock in DistroKid. Create your account today. Upload "Brand New Me" immediately. DistroKid needs at least 2 weeks to deliver to all stores, so time is tight. Do this first.
  • Set up your pre-save link. Use DistroKid's HyperFollow or a free tool like Linktr.ee to create a page where people can pre-save the track on Spotify and Apple Music before it drops.
  • Claim and optimize Spotify for Artists. Upload a header image, write a real bio (not placeholder text), set your artist pick, and make the profile look legitimate.
  • Claim and optimize Apple Music for Artists. Same deal. Upload images, fill out the profile. These platforms surface artists who look active.
  • Set up a Linktree (or similar). Since you don't have a website yet, this is your central hub. Include pre-save link, Instagram, TikTok, Facebook. Keep it clean.
  • Content audit. Gather every piece of existing footage you have: studio clips, behind-the-scenes moments, photos, homemade TikToks. Put it all in one folder.
  • Plan and batch-shoot teaser content. Film 5 to 7 short-form videos this week. You need content in the bank before the rollout starts heating up. Talking-head clips, audio snippets over visuals, raw moments.
  • Prepare a press kit. One page with your bio, a high-quality photo, the cover art, and a short description of the song. You will need this for playlist pitching and media outreach.
Phase 2
Tease & Build
April 6 - April 13
The foundation is built. Now you start putting heat behind it. This week is about creating anticipation and planting seeds with your community.
  • Drop Teaser #1. A 15-second audio snippet over a cinematic visual or designed lyric card. Caption should hook the story: "3 years in the making. April 20."
  • Post the story behind the song. A text post or talking-head video about what "Brand New Me" really means. This is where the 10-year journey becomes the hook. Be honest. The story sells the song.
  • Share a behind-the-scenes studio moment. Something raw. The session, a vocal take, a moment with the producer. People want to see the process.
  • Activate your church community. Let your pastor or worship leader know the single is coming. Ask if they would be willing to share or mention it. Your faith community is your most loyal base.
  • Reach out to creator/influencer connections. You mentioned knowing a few people. Ask them now to be ready to share on release day. Give them the pre-save link.
  • Run a small ad ($50-75). Boost the teaser post on Instagram and Facebook. Target males 18-34 in Tennessee who follow country/rock artists. Keep it focused.
  • Daily stories. Countdown content, lyric snippets, process clips. Stay visible. The algorithm rewards consistency.
  • Engage hard. Comment on posts from artists in your lane. Reply to every comment on your content. This is free growth.
Phase 3
Pre-Release Push
April 14 - April 19
Final countdown. Everything ramps up here. You are building so much anticipation that when April 20 hits, people are already looking for it.
  • Pitch to Spotify editorial playlists. Go into Spotify for Artists, use the playlist pitching tool, and submit "Brand New Me" for editorial consideration. Do this 7+ days before release (which means immediately).
  • Pitch to independent playlist curators. Find country, indie, southern rock, Christian, and faith-based playlists on Spotify. DM the curators or use SubmitHub ($10-20 in credits) to submit directly.
  • Drop Teaser #2. A different snippet this time, ideally the hook. Link to pre-save in bio. Make it clear the song is almost here.
  • "5 Days Until Brand New Me" countdown series. One post per day counting down. Each one should reveal something new: a lyric, a story, a clip. Keep building the tension.
  • Share the quotable lyric as a designed graphic. "I finally made it to the other side of shame." Make this visually striking. Gold text, dark background, minimal. Let it hit.
  • Ask close friends and family to pre-save and share. Text them directly. Give them the link. Tell them what it would mean.
  • Prepare release day content. Have your announcement post, story sequence, and a reel with the best 30 seconds ready to go. Do not scramble on release morning.
  • If the music video is happening, film it now. If a full video is not in the budget, consider a visualizer: the cover art with subtle motion, or performance footage cut together.
  • Run second ad push ($50-75). Target pre-save conversions this time. Point people directly to the HyperFollow link.
  • Contact local media. Podcasts, blogs, local news. The comeback story is the angle. Tennessee artist, former baseball player, decade of change, new single. That is a story people want to tell.
  • Sunday, April 19: Church mention. April 20 is a Monday. Ask your church to mention the release during Sunday service or in the bulletin. This is your strongest community.
Phase 4
Release Day
April 20, 2026
This is it. Everything has been building to this day. Your job is to make as much noise as possible and make it impossible for people to miss.
  • Post the official announcement at 12:01 AM or early morning. "Brand New Me is out everywhere." Include streaming links to Spotify, Apple Music, and everywhere else.
  • Share streaming links across every platform. Instagram feed post, Instagram stories, TikTok, Facebook. Multiple posts, not just one.
  • Pin the release post on Instagram and TikTok so it is the first thing anyone sees when they visit your profile.
  • Go live on Instagram. Even if it is 15 minutes. Play the song, talk about what it means, thank people for the support. Lives get pushed by the algorithm.
  • Text and DM everyone personally. Friends, family, supporters, church community. A personal message hits different from a mass story post. "Hey, my single just dropped. Would mean the world if you gave it a listen and shared it."
  • Ask supporters to add to personal playlists. This is one of the most underrated moves. Every playlist add signals Spotify that the song is connecting.
  • Post multiple times throughout the day. Morning announcement, midday reminder, evening gratitude post. Do not post once and disappear.
  • Engage with every single comment and share. Every one. This is the day to be glued to your phone.
Phase 5
Post-Release Momentum
April 21 - May 4
Most independent artists stop promoting after release day. That is a mistake. The two weeks after the drop are where you either fade or build real traction.
  • Share streaming milestones. First 1,000 streams, first playlist add, anything that shows movement. People want to support winners. Show them the song is working.
  • Post fan reactions and messages. Screenshots of DMs, comments, stories from people who connected with the song. Social proof is everything.
  • Create "what this line means to me" content. Take specific lyrics and make short-form videos breaking down the meaning. This stretches one song into multiple pieces of content.
  • Repurpose the song for TikTok trends and reels. Use the hook over relatable content. The more people hear it, the more it sticks.
  • Continue playlist pitching. Independent curators accept submissions all the time. Keep submitting to country, indie, and faith playlists weekly.
  • Explore booking a local show. A release show or even an open mic set within 30 days of release keeps the energy alive and gives you content for weeks.
  • Use remaining budget for targeted ads. If the numbers look strong, put the last dollars behind whatever content is performing best.
  • Pitch to local podcasts and blogs. The comeback story is timeless. Podcasters love an underdog narrative.
  • Start teasing "what's next." Do not let the conversation end with one song. Even a hint that more music is coming keeps people paying attention.

05 / Social Media Playbook

Content Strategy & Engagement

Four content pillars, a posting rhythm, and engagement tactics that actually work for independent artists.

🎸
The Sound
Audio snippets, studio clips, vocal takes, beat breakdowns. Let people hear the music in raw form before they hear the polished version. Tease the hook, the bridge, the opening bars.
📜
The Story
The 10-year journey. The bad habits, the turning point, the transformation. This is what makes "Brand New Me" more than a song. People connect with honesty. Tell yours.
🎥
The Grind
Behind the scenes. The studio sessions, the long nights, the process of making music independently. Show people what it actually takes. This builds respect and investment.
✝️
The Faith
Your testimony angle. This is not about preaching. It is about being real about where you were and where you are now. Your church community is your foundation. Lean into it.
Posting Times
When to Post
For a 20s-and-up audience on Instagram, TikTok, and Facebook:
  • Instagram Reels/Feed: Tuesday through Friday, 11am to 1pm or 7pm to 9pm. Avoid Monday mornings and late nights.
  • TikTok: Tuesday through Thursday, 10am to 12pm or 7pm to 9pm. TikTok is more flexible. Post and see what sticks.
  • Facebook: Wednesday through Friday, 1pm to 4pm. Facebook skews slightly older. Great for the faith community content.
  • Stories: Throughout the day. Morning, midday, evening. Stories are low-pressure and high-frequency.
Hashtag Strategy
Tags That Work
Mix broad reach with niche discovery. Never use more than 15 hashtags on Instagram. TikTok is more forgiving.
  • Broad reach: #NewMusic #IndependentArtist #CountryMusic #NewSingle #MusicRelease
  • Genre specific: #SouthernRock #IndieCountry #CountryRock #TennesseeMusic #NashvilleSound
  • Story driven: #Comeback #Transformation #BrandNew #NewChapter #FaithJourney
  • Artist specific: #BL4INE #BrandNewMe (create these and use them on every post so they build over time)
Engagement Tactics
Growth Moves
  • Reply to every comment within the first hour. The algorithm prioritizes posts with fast engagement. Your reply counts as a comment too, doubling your count.
  • Comment on 10 to 15 similar artists' posts daily. Not spam. Real comments. "This is fire" does not count. Write something that shows you actually watched or listened.
  • Use question stickers and polls in stories. "What is your favorite line from the teaser?" or "Spotify or Apple Music?" Gets people tapping.
  • DM people who share your content. Thank them personally. This turns casual supporters into loyal fans.
  • Duet and stitch other TikToks in your genre. React to country music content, respond to songwriting prompts. Borrow their audience.
