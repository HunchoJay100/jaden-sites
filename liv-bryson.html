What to Wear

Dress Code

Semi-formal · Garden elegant
👗

Ladies

Cocktail dresses, midi or maxi dresses, elegant jumpsuits. Think garden party meets elegance. Avoid white or ivory.

👔

Gentlemen

Dress shirts with slacks or chinos. Sport coat optional but encouraged. No ties required. Polished but comfortable.

The venue is outdoors on a farm — consider comfortable shoes for grass and gravel.

Where to Stay

Accommodations

The Gravatt Farm

The Gravatt family will be hosting guests at their beautiful farm property in Cookeville. If you're traveling in from out of town, accommodations are available on-site so you can enjoy the full weekend without worrying about the drive.
