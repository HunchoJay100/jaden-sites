Types of Policies

~12 questions on exam
■ Term Life
  • Pure protection, no cash value
  • Types: Level Term, Decreasing Term, Annual Renewable Term (ART), Convertible Term
  • Cheapest form of life insurance
  • Renewable = can renew without proof of insurability (premium increases at each renewal)
  • Convertible = can convert to permanent policy without proof of insurability
■ Whole Life (Permanent)
  • Level premium, guaranteed cash value, coverage to age 100/121
  • Types: Straight/Ordinary Life, Limited-Pay (20-Pay, Life Paid-Up at 65), Single Premium, Modified (lower premiums early, higher later), Graded Premium
  • Limited-Pay builds cash value FASTER (higher premiums, fewer years)
■ Universal Life
  • Flexible premiums, adjustable death benefit
  • Two components: insurance (ART) + cash account
  • Interest-sensitive (current rate, guaranteed minimum)
  • Target premium = suggested amount to keep policy in force for life
■ Variable Life
  • Fixed premium, investment subaccounts (stocks, bonds, mutual funds)
  • Policyholder bears investment risk
  • REQUIRES securities license (Series 6 or 7) to sell
  • Cash value and death benefit fluctuate with market
■ Variable Universal Life (VUL)
  • Flexible premium + investment subaccounts
  • REQUIRES securities license
  • Most flexible and most risky
■ Indexed Universal Life (IUL)
  • Cash value tied to market index (S&P 500)
  • Has a floor (0% minimum) and a cap
  • Does NOT require securities license
Quick Hit: Securities License

Variable Life + Variable Universal Life + Variable Annuities = REQUIRES securities license.
Indexed products (IUL, Indexed Annuity) do NOT require one.

■ Annuities
  • Accumulation phase = money grows tax-deferred
  • Annuitization phase = payout begins
  • Fixed = guaranteed rate, insurance company bears risk, inflation risk to owner
  • Variable = subaccounts, owner bears risk, REQUIRES securities license
  • Indexed = tied to index with floor/cap
  • Immediate = payments start within 12 months (funded with single premium)
  • Deferred = payments start later
■ Combination Plans
  • Joint Life (First-to-Die) = pays on first death
  • Survivorship / Second-to-Die = pays on second death (used for estate planning)

Provisions, Riders & Options

~19 questions on exam · BIGGEST SECTION
📚 Key Provisions (MEMORIZE THESE)
ProvisionWhat It Does
Grace Period31 days to pay overdue premium. Coverage continues. If insured dies, benefit paid minus unpaid premium.
IncontestabilityAfter 2 years, insurer cannot contest claims for misstatements in application.
Misstatement of Age/GenderNEVER incontestable. Benefits adjusted to what premium would have bought at correct age.
Entire ContractPolicy + application = entire contract. Nothing outside can modify it.
ReinstatementTypically within 3-5 years. Must pay back premiums + interest, provide evidence of insurability.
Suicide Clause2 years. If suicide within 2 years, only premiums returned (no death benefit).
AssignmentAbsolute = full transfer of ownership. Collateral = temporary, for a loan.
Automatic Premium LoanUses cash value to pay overdue premium automatically if elected.
Free Look10 days standard / 20 days for replacement policies in TN.
💰 The 3 Nonforfeiture Options

When you stop paying premiums on a cash value policy:

  1. Cash Surrender Value - take the cash and walk away
  2. Reduced Paid-Up Insurance - lower face amount, paid up for life, no more premiums
  3. Extended Term Insurance - SAME face amount, but term coverage only (limited time)
🧠 Memory Trick
"Cash, Reduce, Extend" = CRE
🌱 Dividend Options (Participating Policies) - PARR-O
  1. Paid-up Additions - buy small amounts of additional paid-up whole life
  2. Accumulate at Interest - leave with insurer, earn interest (THE INTEREST IS TAXABLE)
  3. Reduce Premium - apply dividend to reduce next premium
  4. Receive as Cash - get a check (dividends are TAX-FREE, they're return of premium)
  5. One-year Term - use dividend to buy 1-year term insurance

Application, Underwriting & Delivery

~11 questions on exam
Quick Hit

Insurable interest must exist AT TIME OF APPLICATION (not at death).
You always have insurable interest in yourself. Also: spouse, parent/child, business partner, key employee, creditor (up to debt amount).

⚖ Contract Law Elements (COLAC)
  • Competent parties
  • Offer and acceptance
  • Legal purpose
  • Adequate consideration (premium + application statements)
  • Contract characteristics:
CharacteristicMeaning
AdhesionTake it or leave it. Ambiguity favors the insured.
AleatoryUnequal exchange (small premiums can yield large benefit)
UnilateralOnly insurer makes an enforceable promise
Utmost Good FaithBoth parties must act honestly
📋 Premium Receipts
  • Conditional Receipt: Coverage begins on date of application or last medical exam (whichever is later), IF applicant is found insurable
  • Binding Receipt: Coverage begins IMMEDIATELY, regardless of insurability (rare)
📈 Underwriting Risk Classes
  1. Preferred (best rates)
  2. Standard
  3. Substandard/Rated (higher premiums or exclusions)
  4. Declined
🔍 Sources of Information
  • MIB (Medical Information Bureau) - database to prevent fraud across companies
  • APS (Attending Physician Statement) - medical records
  • Inspection reports
  • Credit reports
Quick Hit: Replacement Rules

Agent must determine if replacement is involved. Must provide comparison forms. Replacing insurer must provide 20-day free look in TN.

Quick Hit: MEC (Modified Endowment Contract)
  • ■ Fails the 7-PAY TEST (overfunded relative to death benefit)
  • ■ Single Premium Whole Life is ALWAYS a MEC
  • ■ Loans and withdrawals taxed LIFO (Last In, First Out = gains come out first)
  • ■ 10% penalty tax if under age 59½
  • ■ Death benefit still income-tax-free

Tennessee-Specific Laws

~18 questions on exam · 26% OF YOUR SCORE
⚠ Do Not Skip This Section

This section is 26% of your exam score. Almost 1 in 4 questions come from here.

🏛 Regulatory Authority
  • Tennessee Dept of Commerce and Insurance
  • Commissioner has authority to examine insurers, enforce laws, discipline producers
📄 Producer Licensing
  • Must be 18+
  • Must be TN resident for resident license
  • Must pass written exam (NO prelicensing education required by TN)
  • Apply through NIPR, $50 + fees
  • Fingerprinting + background check via IdentoGo
  • Report address change within 30 days
⏰ Temporary License
  • Up to 180 days, NO exam required
  • ONLY for: death/disability of licensed producer (spouse/court rep), employee of business entity, military service designee
  • Commissioner may require licensed sponsor
📚 Continuing Education
RequirementDetails
CE Hours24 hours every 2 years
Ethics3 hours included in the 24
Renewal DeadlineLast day of birth month, biennially
Annuity SuitabilityOne-time 4-hour course
Long-Term CareOne-time 8-hour course + 4-hour renewal every 24 months
Flood InsuranceOne-time 3-hour course
🕑 Free Look Periods
  • 10 days for standard policies
  • 20 days for replacement policies
🕑 Grace Periods
  • 7 days for weekly premium
  • 10 days for monthly premium
  • 31 days for all other policies
🚫 Unfair Trade Practices (TN Code 56-8-104)
  • Misrepresentation
  • False advertising
  • Defamation of competitors
  • Boycott, coercion, intimidation
  • Unfair discrimination
  • Rebating (sharing commission as inducement)
  • Twisting (misrepresentation to induce replacement)
  • Commissioner issues cease and desist orders
Quick Hit: Know These Definitions
TwistingMisrepresentation to replace existing policy (DECEPTIVE)
ChurningExcessive replacement for commission (similar to twisting but repeated)
RebatingOffering inducements not in the policy (illegal in TN)
CoercionForcing someone to buy insurance as condition of a loan/service

Practice Questions

28 questions · test yourself
Question 1
Which type of life insurance provides coverage for a specified period and does NOT build cash value?
A) Whole life B) Universal life C) Term life D) Endowment
Answer: C) Term life
Question 2
Which policy has flexible premiums and an adjustable death benefit?
A) Whole life B) Variable life C) Universal life D) Term life
Answer: C) Universal life
Question 3
Which type requires a securities license to sell?
A) Universal life B) Indexed universal life C) Variable life D) Whole life
Answer: C) Variable life
Question 4
Which whole life policy accumulates cash value faster in early years?
A) Straight Life B) 20-Pay Life C) Modified Whole Life D) Graded Premium
Answer: B) 20-Pay Life
Question 5
A Single Premium Whole Life policy would most likely be classified as?
A) Term policy B) MEC C) Variable policy D) Annuity
Answer: B) Modified Endowment Contract (fails 7-pay test)
Question 6
During which phase of an annuity does money grow tax-deferred?
A) Annuitization B) Accumulation C) Distribution D) Surrender
Answer: B) Accumulation phase
Question 7
The grace period for a life insurance policy in Tennessee is:
A) 10 days B) 21 days C) 31 days D) 60 days
Answer: C) 31 days (for other than weekly/monthly premium)
Question 8
After 2 years, the insurer cannot deny a claim based on application misstatements. This is:
A) Entire Contract B) Grace Period C) Incontestability D) Misstatement of Age
Answer: C) Incontestability clause
Question 9
If the insured's age was misstated, what happens?
A) Policy voided B) Claim denied C) Benefit adjusted D) Premium refunded
Answer: C) Benefit adjusted to what premium would have purchased at correct age
Question 10
Which is NOT a nonforfeiture option?
A) Cash surrender B) Reduced paid-up C) Extended term D) Paid-up additions
Answer: D) Paid-up additions (that's a dividend option)
Question 11
The nonforfeiture option providing same face amount as term coverage is:
A) Reduced paid-up B) Extended term C) Cash surrender D) APL
Answer: B) Extended term
Question 12
Which dividend option results in taxable income?
A) Cash B) Reduce premium C) Accumulate at interest D) Paid-up additions
Answer: C) Accumulate at interest (the interest earned is taxable)
Question 13
Which settlement option provides highest periodic payment?
A) Fixed period B) Fixed amount C) Life income (straight) D) Joint and survivor
Answer: C) Life income/straight life (highest payment but stops at death)
Question 14
Which rider waives premiums if insured becomes disabled?
A) Guaranteed insurability B) ADB C) Waiver of premium D) Payor benefit
Answer: C) Waiver of premium
Question 15
Insurable interest must exist at what point?
A) Time of claim B) Time of death C) Time of application D) Time of delivery
Answer: C) Time of application
Question 16
Which organization prevents fraud across insurance companies?
A) NAIC B) MIB C) FINRA D) SEC
Answer: B) MIB (Medical Information Bureau)
Question 17
A conditional receipt means coverage begins:
A) When policy delivered B) When approved C) Date of application/last exam if insurable D) 30 days after
Answer: C) On date of application or last medical exam, IF found insurable
Question 18
Life insurance death benefits to a named beneficiary are:
A) Fully taxable B) Tax-free C) Capital gains D) Partially taxable
Answer: B) Tax-free (IRC Section 101(a))
Question 19
Life insurance premiums paid by an individual are:
A) Deductible B) Not deductible C) Deductible up to $5K D) Deductible if itemized
Answer: B) Not tax deductible
Question 20
A MEC is created when a policy fails what test?
A) 5-pay B) 7-pay C) 10-pay D) Corridor
Answer: B) 7-pay test
Question 21
Per stirpes distribution means:
A) Split equally among living B) Deceased's share to their heirs C) Goes to estate D) Contingent only
Answer: B) Deceased beneficiary's share passes to their children/descendants
Question 22
Group life conversion privilege allows how many days?
A) 10 B) 30 C) 31 D) 60
Answer: C) 31 days to convert to individual whole life without proof of insurability
Question 23
Misrepresentation to induce someone to replace an existing policy is:
A) Rebating B) Churning C) Twisting D) Defamation
Answer: C) Twisting
Question 24
Sharing commission as inducement to buy is:
A) Twisting B) Rebating C) Churning D) Coercion
Answer: B) Rebating
Question 25
TN temporary license is valid for up to:
A) 30 days B) 90 days C) 180 days D) 1 year
Answer: C) 180 days
Question 26
TN CE requirement is:
A) 20 hrs/year B) 24 hrs/2 years C) 30 hrs/2 years D) 24 hrs/year
Answer: B) 24 hours every 2 years including 3 hours ethics
Question 27
Free look period for replacement policies in TN:
A) 10 days B) 15 days C) 20 days D) 30 days
Answer: C) 20 days
Question 28
Cash value inside a life insurance policy grows:
A) Tax-free B) Tax-deferred C) Fully taxable D) Capital gains
Answer: B) Tax-deferred
