The Itinerary

Friday Night — April 10
Roll Into Nashville
Drive down from Cookeville (~1.5 hrs). Check in, get settled, maybe hit Broadway for a warm-up round. Early to bed — race day is a daytime event.
Saturday 6:30 AM — Wake Up
Coffee & Lock In
Fuel up. Grab breakfast on the way. Nissan Stadium parking opens early — beat the crowd.
Saturday 7:00 AM — 1:00 PM
FanFest & Qualifying
This is where it gets real. Watch qualifying runs, walk the pits, get autographs and photos with riders. Team rigs, bike displays, partner activations. FanFest ticket required (on top of event ticket).
Saturday 1:30 PM
Opening Ceremonies
Fireworks. Flames. Monster Energy does not play. Find your seats, strap in.
Saturday 2:00 PM
Gate Drop — Main Events
250SX East heat races → 250 Main → 450SX heat races → 450 Main Event. 20 laps of chaos. Whoops, rhythm sections, and roosting at full send. This is what we came for.
Saturday ~6:00 PM
Post-Race Dinner
Head to one of the spots below. Talk about the race. Argue about who sent it hardest. Eat like kings.
Saturday Night
Nashville After Dark
Broadway is right there. Live music, cold drinks, and the energy of 60,000 moto fans who just watched the best in the world throw down. Or just cruise back to Cookeville on the high of the day.
The Arena

Nissan Stadium

Where Dirt Meets Downtown

Home of the Tennessee Titans, turned into a full-blown Supercross track for one night. 69,143 seats. Dirt hauled in by the truckload. The track is built inside the stadium bowl — steep whoops, nasty rhythm lanes, and a start straight that'll give you chills when those gates drop.

Nissan Stadium sits right on the Cumberland River across from downtown Nashville. Walking distance to Broadway, the honky-tonks, and everything else.

1 Titans Way, Nashville TN 37213 69,143 Capacity Parking Opens Early Round 13 of 17

Where We're Eating

Skull's Rainbow Room

Fine Dining • Jazz • Cocktails

Nashville legend since 1948. Hidden in the basement of Printers Alley. Live jazz, craft cocktails, and a dinner menu that's been recognized by Condé Nast. It's the kind of place you go after watching guys send 70-foot triples. Dress sharp.

222 Printers Alley • (615) 810-9631 • Reservations recommended

Acme Feed & Seed

Southern • Rooftop • Live Music

22,000 sq ft of food, drinks, and live music right on Broadway. Rooftop bar with a direct view of Nissan Stadium and the Cumberland River. Repeatedly voted Nashville's best rooftop. Perfect for a post-race vibe.

101 Broadway • (615) 915-0888 • Walk-ins welcome

Black Tap

Craft Burgers • Shakes • Casual

Award-winning burgers in the Gulch. Zagat & TimeOut recognized. Get the All-American burger and one of the insane CrazyShakes. No frills, just elite-level burgers after an elite-level race.
