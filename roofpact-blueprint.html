The Big Picture

What RoofPact is

Roofing companies pay you to run their membership programs. Homeowners pay the roofing company for annual roof protection. You take $4/member/month. The homeowner never sees RoofPact.

The Problem

Feast or famine

Roofing companies live on one-time jobs. No recurring revenue, no customer retention, no predictability. One slow month and payroll is a problem.

The Solution

Branded memberships

Annual roof protection memberships sold under the roofing company's brand. Inspections, priority service, repair discounts. Homeowners pay yearly, company earns recurring revenue.

The Business

White-label SaaS

$1,000 one-time setup per company. $4/active member/month via Stripe Connect. One codebase serves every company. The more companies and members, the more you make.

Who Uses What

Four interfaces, four user types

Each user type has their own login, their own UI, and their own level of access.

User Type 1

Visitors (B2B)

Roofing company owners browsing roofpact.com. They see the marketing pitch, pricing ($1K setup + $4/member/mo), and submit a demo request form.

Entry: roofpact.com
Auth: None required
User Type 2

Homeowners

A roofing company's customers. They land on roofpact.com/[slug] and see a fully branded page with their roofer's colors, logo, program name, and tier pricing.

Entry: roofpact.com/mrg-roofing (example)
Portal: /account (magic link login)
Sees: Dashboard, inspections, health score, membership, transfer
User Type 3

Company Admins

The roofing company's staff. They manage their members, schedule inspections, complete inspections with photos/scores, generate PDF reports, and track revenue.

Entry: roofpact.com/login
Auth: Email + password
Dashboard: /admin (stats, members, schedule, analytics, settings)
User Type 4

Platform Admin (You)

Jaden. Master control panel. Onboard new companies, edit their branding/pricing, track demo requests, see all companies at a glance.

Entry: roofpact.com/login (same page, role-based redirect)
Auth: Email + password (platform_admin role)
Dashboard: /platform (overview, companies, onboarding, demo requests)

Key Architecture Decisions
01 Multi-tenant via slugs. roofpact.com/[slug] renders any company's branded page from one template.
02 Stripe Connect (planned). Money flows through RoofPact, auto-splits to company. No manual invoicing.
03 No manual member creation. Members only created via Stripe webhook on payment (prevents bypassing platform fees).
04 Magic link for members. Low-frequency login (check reports once a year). Password for admins (daily use).
05 PDF reports branded per company. Pulls company name, colors, contact from database. Not hardcoded.

Before You Go Live

Things to think about

Items that need attention before onboarding a real paying company.

Critical

Stripe Connect

No payment code exists. Companies can't collect from homeowners and you can't collect your $4/member. This is the single biggest blocker to revenue.

Important

Verify Email Domain

noreply@roofpact.com needs to be verified in Resend. Without it, emails may land in spam. Takes 5 minutes in the Resend dashboard.

Important

Member Portal Branding

When a member logs in at /account, they see RoofPact branding, not their roofing company's. Needs to pull company colors/logo dynamically.

Nice to Have

Password Reset

Company admins have no way to reset their password if they forget it. Supabase has this built in, just needs a UI page.

Nice to Have

Mobile Responsiveness

The app hasn't been audited for mobile. Admin dashboard and member portal should work on phones for field use.

Consider

RLS Policy Audit

Row-level security is enabled on all tables but policies should be reviewed to make sure no data leaks between tenants.
