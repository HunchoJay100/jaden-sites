Setup

How it works

Five steps. Ten minutes. After this you've got a personal trainer + nutrition coach that lives in your terminal and knows everything about how Jaden coaches.

1
Clone the repo
Accept the GitHub invite first (check your email). Then in Terminal: git clone https://github.com/HunchoJay100/GET-MATT-JACKED.git ~/GET-MATT-JACKED
2
Verify everything's there
cd ~/GET-MATT-JACKED && ./verify.sh · checks every file is in place and you're good to go.
3
Set up the coach shortcut
So you can launch the trainer from anywhere on your machine without remembering paths. Pick one:

Easy way · run the script:
cd ~/GET-MATT-JACKED && ./setup-alias.sh
It auto-detects your shell and adds the alias for you.

Manual way · add this to ~/.zshrc:
coach() { (cd ~/GET-MATT-JACKED && git fetch --quiet 2>/dev/null && git merge --ff-only --quiet 2>/dev/null; claude "$@") }
Then close and reopen Terminal.

Either version of this auto-pulls Jaden's latest changes every time you launch · the brain stays current without you doing anything.
4
Type coach and start
From any Terminal window, anywhere on your machine, type coach and hit enter. The brain auto-loads. Tell it "I'm new, let's run intake" · it walks you through stats, confirms your track, drafts your v1 plan.
5
Send Jaden the draft
Coach saves the plan as plans/_draft-for-jaden/matt-{track}-v1-DRAFT.md. Text Jaden the path. He reviews, tweaks, and ships v1. Then you train.
Every day after that
Just type coach from anywhere. The brain reloads automatically every session · no setup, no cd, no thinking. Coach already knows your profile, your plan, your last session. Just talk.
Custom build

Built for your life

Not a generic fitness app. The brain is loaded with the constraints and preferences that make this work for the version of you that's on a shoot at 11pm in a hotel gym.

Knee-aware coaching
Plans ship as designed. If your knee acts up, coach acknowledges it (you don't have to re-explain), lists 2-3 substitution options, and lets you pick. Plus reminds you to log the pain so Jaden can adjust the next version.
Gluten-free, every food
Every food on your list is gluten-free. Coach knows the hidden landmines (soy sauce, malt vinegar, breaded everything). Restaurant scripts and gas station survival picks are all gluten-free defaults.
Shoot-day ready
30, 45, or 60 minute sessions. Cut a session short mid-workout, coach re-plans the rest. Hotel gym, no rack, just dumbbells · the substitution map handles it. Your shoot weeks don't break the streak.
Eat your way
You eat what you want. Coach hits the numbers around your real life. Hard rails are 2,020 cal and 170g protein. Carbs and fat are flex · high-carb training day, low-carb steakhouse night, whatever. Coach just keeps the running total.

The two rules

What stays constant

Most of this is flexible. These two are not. They're the load-bearing rules behind why this works.

Rule 01
No plan goes live without Jaden's eyes
Coach drafts every plan into plans/_draft-for-jaden/. Jaden reviews and promotes to plans/active/. Coach never ships you something untested. The repo is alpha · this gate is the QA. Send Jaden the draft path, wait for the green light, then go.
Rule 02
You pick your weight, every set
Coach never auto-prescribes load. Coach shows you last week's numbers ("you did 185 for 7") and your job is to beat it. Logbook progression is the coaching mechanism. The numbers climb because you climb them, not because the app told you to.
