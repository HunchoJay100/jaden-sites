Diagnosis

What's Going Wrong

  1. Creative fatigue is your #1 issue. Frequency at 3.4 and rising. The algorithm is running out of fresh audience to show your current ads to.
  2. Budget is misallocated by ad type. 64% of spend is going to phone promos ($20.34/lead) when page promos are delivering at $8.83/lead.
  3. Feedback Loop: Accidental taps are poisoning data. Call-objective ads + 65+ users accidentally tapping = phantom conversions. Meta optimizes for more of the same. Switch to lead form objectives.
  4. Instagram is a reach play, not a conversion play. $207/week for a 0.83% CTR is inefficient for lead gen.
  5. One ad is carrying the team. The $39.27 page promo with 14 leads at $2.81 is doing more than campaigns spending 2x as much.
