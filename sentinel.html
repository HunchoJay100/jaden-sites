The Pipeline

Five stages. Zero intervention.

At 6:00 PM every Friday, Sentinel kicks off a five-stage pipeline. Nobody touches anything. Nobody opens anything. It pulls, calculates, analyzes, renders, and delivers.

01
Connect to JobTread
Sentinel hits the JobTread API and pulls every time entry for the pay week -- Saturday through Friday. Paginated queries sweep through every record. Nothing gets missed.
JobTread API
02
Calculate Payroll
Each entry gets matched to the crew member's wage rate. Per-entry cost, per-job subtotals, and weekly grand totals are calculated automatically. The CSV drops with weekly totals at the top so there's nothing to add up.
Wage Engine
03
AI Intelligence Brief
The full dataset gets fed to an AI analysis engine. It writes the Sentinel Brief -- crew performance, job allocation patterns, efficiency flags, concerns, and actionable recommendations for next week. It compares against prior weeks to surface trends.
AI Engine
04
Render the Report
A branded PDF is generated with the Sentinel Brief, individual employee cards, job allocation breakdowns, and full time log detail. Everything saved automatically to a weekly folder on Jaden's desktop.
Auto-Archived
05
Deliver via iMessage
A text summary with each crew member's hours and total pay hits Jaden's phone. Quick read from anywhere -- full report is waiting on the desktop when he's ready for the deep dive.
iMessage

Intelligence

The Sentinel Brief.

Every report includes an AI-written analysis. Crew performance, job allocation, efficiency patterns, flags, and recommendations. It studies prior weeks to surface trends you'd miss manually.

Sentinel Brief -- Week of Mar 7
OVERVIEW
Week closed at 42.9 hrs / $1,443.83 across 2 crew and 7 jobs.

EFFICIENCY
Hickory Cove received solid attention at 7.5 hrs.
Cliff Heights and Parragon under 1 hr each -- at risk of falling behind.

CREW PERFORMANCE
Jordan -- consistent Mon-Thu, managed oversight across all active sites. Strong GM week.
Gavin -- covered 4 jobs, led field hours at Hickory Cove. Productive range.

RECOMMENDATIONS
1. Confirm Gavin's schedule for next week to avoid site gaps.
2. Prioritize Cliff Heights and Parragon -- both need hours.
