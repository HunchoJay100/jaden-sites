Mission
What Sentinel Does
Sentinel connects directly to JobTread and pulls every time entry for the pay week — Saturday through Friday. It knows who worked, where they worked, when they clocked in and out, and how many hours each job received. Then it does the math, builds the reports, and delivers everything automatically.
01
Time Entry Capture
Pulls every clock-in and clock-out from JobTread for the full pay week. Every minute tracked, every job logged.
02
Wage Calculation
Applies each crew member's actual hourly rate. Calculates per-entry cost, per-job subtotals, and weekly grand totals.
03
AI Analysis
An AI intelligence engine analyzes the data — crew performance, job allocation, efficiency patterns, and actionable recommendations.
04
Auto-Delivery
Text summary hits Jaden's phone at 6pm every Friday. No manual trigger. No reminders needed. It just runs.
Operations
How It Works
Every Friday at 6:00 PM Eastern, a five-stage pipeline executes automatically. No one has to click anything. No one has to open a spreadsheet.
1
Data Pull
Sentinel connects to JobTread's API and pulls every time entry from Saturday through Friday. Paginated queries ensure nothing gets missed — even if there are hundreds of entries.
2
CSV Generation
A clean spreadsheet is built with weekly totals at the top — each person's hours, rate, and total pay at a glance. Below that, every single time entry with job, date, clock in/out, and cost.
3
AI Intelligence Brief
The payroll data gets fed to an AI engine that writes a Sentinel Brief — covering crew performance, job allocation efficiency, concerns, and recommendations for next week. It compares against previous weeks to spot trends.
4
PDF Report
A branded, dark-themed PDF report is rendered with the Sentinel Brief, employee breakdowns, job allocation bars, and detailed time logs. Saved to the desktop in a weekly folder for easy reference.
5
Text Delivery
A text summary with everyone's hours and pay hits Jaden's phone via iMessage. Quick snapshot from the field — no need to open a laptop.
