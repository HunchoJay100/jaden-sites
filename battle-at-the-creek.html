What This Builds

THE THREE THINGS
01

Clean Turnover

You deadlift 500+ and clean ~255. The gap is speed under the bar, not strength. Fix the turnover and the 1RM climbs — doubles as faster 1776 cycling.

02

Barbell Endurance

155 C&J and 225 deadlift feeling submaximal when you're already tired. Trained by reps-per-set and the reset rhythm, not by grinding.

03

Multi-Event Reps

Two full sims so comp day isn't the first time you feel event 3 on event-1 legs. Everything else is already comp-ready and maintained through class.

Shoulder

Get it assessed Phase 1 — book it now, don't let it drift. Cuff rehab daily, 10 min, non-negotiable (both ends on clean days). Ropes stay parked until cleared. Clean work only when fresh — the second bar speed drops or a catch gets ugly, you're done for the day.

Bike — Thu Ceiling, Sun Floor

Drive the 50-cal Echo time down. Under 1:30 needs 85+ RPM held. Rotate Thursday weekly; Sunday stays aerobic.

  • WEEK A · ceiling — 8 × 15 sec HARD @ 85-90 RPM / 75 sec easy
  • WEEK B · sustain — 5 × 10 cal for time / rest 90 sec, chunk 5 ≥ chunk 1
  • WEEK C · race pace — 3 × 20 cal / rest 2-3 min, open ~80, negative-split
  • Every Thu: WU + 3 × 10-sec cadence bursts, then the set, then 20-30 min Z2
  • Every Sun: aerobic ride + optional 2-3 × 3-5 min sustained-cadence blocks
  • Once a month: test an all-out fresh 50-cal. Watch the number drop.

Standing Rules

THE GUARDRAILS
Class is the baseDon't bolt a second program on top. We hijack existing slots — 4 classes, 1 trainer session, 1-2 solo focus slots. That's it.
Clean only when freshTurnover done tired grooves slow reps. Bar speed drops or the catch gets ugly? Done for the day. No exceptions.
Stay-after logicDo class warm-up + strength as programmed, then either the class metcon (if it maps to your events) or your comp-specific piece.
Ease before testsPull Tuesday's clean volume back the week of the Wk 7 DL benchmark and both sims. Treat those like mini-tests — show up fresh.
