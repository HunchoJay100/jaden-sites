About Richter Technology

We started this company because
the Upper Cumberland deserved better IT.

Most IT providers in this area are built for small office setups. There was nobody who could handle a 500-workstation network, manage a server migration without downtime, or architect infrastructure that actually scales. So we built that company.

  • We handle what others can't

    Networks up to 1,000 workstations. Multi-location environments. Complex server architectures. We're one of the only firms in the region with the capability to operate at this scale.

  • Engineers, not technicians

    There's a difference between restarting a service and architecting a solution. Our team holds certifications across major platforms and designs infrastructure from the ground up.

  • We work with what you have

    We don't force you to rip and replace. We'll manage your network regardless of who installed it, what brand it is, or how old the equipment is. If it works, we'll keep it working. If it doesn't, we'll tell you why.

  • We catch problems before you do

    Our monitoring and managed services platform watches your infrastructure around the clock. When something starts to drift, we're already on it. You find out it was handled, not that it happened.
