## Aesthetic
- Dark themes with gold accents
- Glass-morphism effects (subtle blur, translucent surfaces)
- Military/tactical feel for operations tools (Chief, Watchdog, Sentinel)
- Clean professional feel for client-facing pages (proposals, BidKit)
- Generous negative space
- Subtle animations (pulse dots, scan lines, gradient glows)
