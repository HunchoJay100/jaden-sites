There's no structure, no visibility, no accountability. Paperclip is the layer that turns those disconnected tools into an operation.

Full Power

API Tokens

Pay per token

API tokens are keys from AI providers (Anthropic, OpenAI, Google) that let your code call models directly. You pay per token (roughly per word) in and out. This is how production AI systems work. A typical agent task costs a few cents.

  • ✓ Mix models: Claude + GPT + Gemini
  • ✓ Unlimited parallel agents
  • ✓ No rate limit bottlenecks
  • ✓ OpenRouter: one key, 200+ models
  • ✓ Per-agent, per-task cost tracking
  • ✓ Production-grade infrastructure
Multi-Model Teams

When you go API, you're not locked to one model. You assign the best model to each role. That's the real unlock.

Strategy

Speed

Content

The Opportunity
You're Building a Second Business Inside Your Business

This is the part that should make you stop and think. What Paperclip actually enables is something much bigger than task automation.

The Concept

An AI Workforce Running Parallel to Your Human Team

Think about what's actually happening here. You're defining roles, assigning responsibilities, setting budgets, and managing output. That's a company. It just happens to be staffed by AI agents. They wake up on schedules, do real work, hand off tasks to each other, and report results. Your human team focuses on the high-leverage stuff. Your AI team handles the volume. Two workforces, one operation, and the AI side scales without adding payroll.

Where This is Headed

Agents Paying Agents

Right now, agents do work and you pay for the compute. But the trajectory is clear: agents that can transact with each other. Your content agent needs a graphic, so it pays a design agent to make one. Your research agent needs specialized data, so it pays an external data agent for access. When agents have budgets and can spend them, they start forming their own supply chains. That's not science fiction. That's where the org chart, budget controls, and governance in Paperclip are already pointing.

The Crypto Connection

Blockchain Was Built for This

When machines need to pay machines, traditional banking doesn't work. No bank is processing a $0.003 transaction between two AI agents at 3am on a Tuesday. But a smart contract will. Crypto and blockchain were designed for exactly this: trustless, programmable, instant payments between parties that don't know each other. Stablecoins for value transfer, smart contracts for service agreements, on-chain records for accountability. The entire crypto infrastructure that's been waiting for a real use case? Autonomous AI agents transacting with each other might be it.

The Bigger Picture

First Movers Build the Infrastructure Everyone Else Rents

The businesses that figure out AI operations now are the ones that will own the playbooks, the workflows, and the institutional knowledge when this becomes standard. Everyone will eventually run AI workforces. The question is whether you're building the infrastructure or renting someone else's. Paperclip is open source. It runs on your machines. You own everything. That matters when this becomes the default way businesses operate.
