Small problems become big bills. Fast.

Your roof doesn't fail all at once. It fails quietly, over months, in places you can't see. By the time you notice, the damage is done.

01

Flashing Failures

The seals around chimneys, skylights, and valleys break down silently. One bad storm and water is inside your walls.

02

Material Deterioration

Shingles crack, curl, and shed granules over time. You won't see it from the ground. We see it on every inspection.

03

Ventilation Issues

Poor airflow traps heat and moisture in your attic. That leads to mold, rot, and a shortened roof life.

04

Storm Damage

Hail and wind leave damage you can't always see. Without documentation, your insurance claim doesn't have a leg to stand on.
