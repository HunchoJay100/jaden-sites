01

What is RoofPact?

An annual membership program where homeowners pay for year-round roof protection. We inspect, we monitor, we respond. They never worry about their roof again.

Recurring Revenue

Every member pays annually. Predictable income that doesn't depend on weather, season, or new leads coming in.

They Call Us First

When a storm hits or something breaks, members don't shop around. They call us because we already know their roof.

Referral Machine

Members refer 2-3x more than one-time customers. Built-in rewards make it systematic, not random.

Insurance Pipeline

Storm inspections uncover claims proactively. We own the replacement pipeline in our market before anyone else even shows up.

09

90-day launch plan

Three phases. Foundation, soft launch, full launch. Goal: 50 members by day 90.

Days 1-30: Foundation

Build the machine

  • Finalize tiers and pricing
  • Build CRM tags and enrollment pipeline
  • Create inspection report template
  • Print enrollment and referral cards
  • Train field techs on enrollment scripts
  • Set up payment processing
Days 31-60: Soft Launch

First 50 members

  • Present RoofPact at every new install close
  • Outbound calls to last 12 months of customers
  • Enroll first 25-50 members
  • Deliver first 10 inspections
  • Refine report template from field feedback
Days 61-90: Full Launch

Open it up

  • Open to full customer base and new leads
  • Activate referral reward system
  • Review conversion rate and fix drop-offs
  • Run Facebook/Google ad campaign
  • Target: 50 enrolled members minimum

The single most important metric in Year 1

Renewal rate. If members are renewing at 70%+, the program is working. If they're not, the inspection experience or communication cadence is the issue.
