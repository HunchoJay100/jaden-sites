What's inside

The Operator engagement, in full.

Twelve months of work, organized into five pillars. Everything below is included at the $25,000 price.

Pillar 01 · The Spine 24 calls · 12 months

Owner Coaching

Two one-on-one coaching calls per month with Jaden for twelve months straight. This is the heart of the engagement, where you learn to actually run a construction company instead of just building houses. The curriculum flexes to where you are, but covers the full operator stack.

  • How we run management, who reports to who, and how to remove yourself from the day-to-day
  • How to set pricing that actually holds margin, by trade and by project type
  • Marketing, leads, sales conversations, and the lifetime value math behind each one
  • Overhead discipline: how we keep ours under $5K a month and how to get yours there
  • Hiring, scaling, and when to bring on a GM versus a second crew
  • Vendor and sub relationships, payment terms, and the leverage you didn't know you had
Pillar 02 · Team 5 hrs live + recorded

Team Training

Five one-hour training calls with your team, recorded so new hires can run through them later. We deploy the methodology across your team and make sure everyone understands how the system runs day to day.

  • Order of Operations across the trades
  • How to run the deliverables (work orders, COIs, lien waivers)
  • Daily and weekly operating rhythm
  • Handoffs, communication standards, and escalation
Pillar 03 · The System First 30 days

JobTread + Methodology Install

In the first month, we deploy our entire operating system inside your business. JobTread set up properly from the ground up, our methodology layered on top, and gaps in your existing process documented and fixed. When the first month closes out, you're running the same stack we run.

  • Full JobTread install: cost codes, templates, workflows, users, permissions
  • Custom Order of Operations adapted to your team and pipeline
  • Deliverables wired into your daily workflow
  • Rebrandable library you can white-label under your own company
  • Process gap audit and remediation plan
Pillar 04 · The Build 14 deliverables

Project Deliverables

Spread across the 12 months and applied to one of your active builds, so you see the system run end to end on a real project. Every deliverable is hands-on, not theoretical.

  • 5 milestone virtual walkthroughs (foundation, framing, MEP rough-in, drywall, final)
  • 6 bid reviews — we tear apart sub bids before you sign them
  • 3 full budget audits — we find leaks, exposure, and overruns before they hit
Pillar 05 · Access 12-mo support · lifetime system

Support & Access

A direct line for the twelve months you're in the engagement, plus the methodology, the portal, and every future update yours to keep forever. The system doesn't expire when the engagement ends.

  • 24-hour text and email response for the full 12 months
  • Lifetime access to the operator-side portal
  • All current and future methodology updates
  • Rebrandable deliverables refreshed as we improve them
Pillar 06 · Optional Add-On Scoped per operator

AI Integration

We run AI heavily inside Flynn Development, and we can install the same layer in your business. Optional, scoped separately, and priced based on what you actually need. Everything from a custom AI assistant trained on your data to JobTread workflow automation and bid-analysis pipelines.

  • Custom AI assistant trained on your operation, builds, and historical data
  • JobTread workflow automation (draws, change orders, daily logs, document generation)
  • Bid and proposal analysis pipelines
  • Lead intake, qualification, and follow-up automation
