Non-Negotiables

Top 10 Wedding Must-Haves

The things you absolutely cannot skip, no matter the budget

1

Venue

Indoor or outdoor, you need a place. This is the single biggest decision that shapes everything else.

2

Officiant

No officiant, no legal marriage. Book early, confirm credentials, and do a rehearsal.

3

Photography

The only vendor that gives you something lasting after the day is over. Don't cut corners here.

4

Catering / Food

Guests will forgive almost anything except being hungry. Feed them well.

5

Music / DJ

Sets the entire mood from ceremony to last dance. A bad DJ kills a reception faster than anything.

6

Florals

Bouquet, ceremony arch, centerpieces. Florals tie the visual theme together. Can be minimal or grand.

7

Wedding Coordinator

Day-of coordinator at minimum. Someone who isn't the bride needs to be running the show. (That's Megan.)

8

Attire

The dress, the suit, alterations. What the couple wears is the visual centerpiece of every photo.

9

Bar / Drinks

Open bar, beer & wine, or signature cocktails. Guests need something to toast with.

10

Restrooms

Especially for outdoor venues. Luxury trailer rentals make it seamless. Don't skip this — guests will notice.
