3 Get to know the project
👂 Let them do the talking. This is a chat, not a survey.

Weave these in naturally. You're listening for: real build, has land, good size, ready soon, talked to a lender.

  1. So tell me what you're thinking. New construction, an addition, or a renovation? Sorts the project type up front
  2. Do you already have a lot or land, or still looking for that piece too? Land means they're a lot closer to ready
  3. Roughly what size are you picturing? A ballpark square footage is fine! Our sweet spot is 2,500 to 4,500 sf
  4. Any idea on the style or finish level you're going for? Reads their budget tier without asking price
  5. Where are you guys located? And are you building right there, or relocating? Confirms the area and catches out-of-town movers
  6. Are you trying to break ground this year, or more just exploring for now? Hot lead vs. keep in touch
  7. Have you talked to a lender yet, or is that still down the road? Financing ready means move them up fast
4 Hand off to Jaden
✨ Slow down here. This is the ask, then let them answer.
The whole point of the call

"This is exactly the kind of project we do, {First}! Let me hand you over to Jaden. He's one of the owners here and he'll walk you through our process and get you guys some numbers."

"He's got some time {day} in the {morning / afternoon}, or {day} works too. Which is better for you?"

  • ●Lock a real day and time. Don't leave it open ended.
  • ●Confirm their best number and let them know Jaden will reach out then.
  • ●Didn't book? All good. They stay in the email follow up automatically, so you're done.
