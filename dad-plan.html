§ 06

The Rules

Non-Negotiables

Protein first. Every meal starts with the protein. If you're short on time, eat the protein and skip the carb before you ever skip the protein.

Don't skip meals. Four meals, every day. Skipping meals slows metabolism and leads to overeating later. Even if you're not hungry, eat.

Water. Minimum a gallon a day. You're training 4 days a week. Dehydration will stall everything.

If the Scale Stalls

If weight doesn't move for 2 weeks straight, here's the order of operations:

1. Add fasted walking. 20-30 min, 3-4 days a week.

2. Add more walking. 5-6 days, or extend to 40 min.

3. Only then do we touch food. And it's a small carb reduction, never protein.

Calories are the last lever. We protect your metabolism, your training, and your sanity in that order.
