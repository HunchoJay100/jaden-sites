The wrong question (price per square foot)

The number one question we get as builders is, "what's your price per square foot?"
And honestly, it's one of the most misleading questions in homebuilding. Price per square foot isn't a number we set. It's what falls out the other end, after every decision you make.
I can build two houses the exact same size, and one costs two hundred grand more than the other. Same square footage. The difference is what you put in it. Your finishes, your foundation, your windows, your kitchen. That's what moves the number. Not the square footage.
So instead of handing you some made-up price per foot, we built something that shows you the truth. You pick your size and the finishes you actually want, and you watch your real build cost move in real time.
It's free, it takes about thirty seconds, and nobody calls you unless you want them to. Go to build dot moderndevelopment dot co and see what your home actually costs to build.
