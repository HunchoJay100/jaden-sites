04 / Nutrition System

90% of
the Results.

The training is the scaffolding. The nutrition system is the building. Three phases, track-aware, built on Jaden's proprietary formulas and validated against his own numbers. The only fitness app where "what should I eat?" has a real answer.

1
Baseline Reset · 14 Days
Clean eating framework. No macros yet. Focus on WHAT, not HOW MUCH. The 14-day clean streak is required to advance. Break it, counter resets. Streak intelligence checks every food log against the rules and flags violations before they cost you the streak. 70–80% processed elimination — sustainability over perfection.
2
Macro Optimization · Unlocked
Jaden's proprietary formula generates your personalized macro targets. Track-aware (Hypertrophy forces surplus, Hard to Kill runs maintenance, etc). Protein floor 150g, ceiling 250g. Calorie floor 1,800, ceiling 4,500. Full barcode scanning, meal templates, favorites, custom meals. Our own MyFitnessPal, built on Open Food Facts (3M+ products, free).
3
Ongoing Adjustment · 16 Rules
16-rule adjustment engine. Daily emoji check-in (hunger/energy/training). Weight trending, macros recalculate automatically. Cardio before cuts — calorie drops are the LAST lever, not the first. The Prime Directive: guys feel BETTER, not worse. Inaction is the default; the app resists tinkering when signals are neutral.
05 / Locked Principles

The Rules That
Don't Flex.

Every decision in the app traces back to one of these. If a feature can't justify itself against these principles, it doesn't ship.

Protein First
Every meal, swap, and recommendation leads with protein. No grilled cheese — there's no protein play.
Older Brother Voice
No "studies show." No "research says." Experience-based, direct, no hedging. This isn't a doctor. It's the older brother who's been there.
Feel Better, Not Worse
The Prime Directive of the adjustment engine. If a plan is tanking energy, mood, or training, the plan is wrong — not the user.
Cardio Before Cuts
Refeed → cardio → more cardio → THEN a tiny cut. Calorie drops are the last lever, not the first. Most apps get this exactly backwards.
Respect the Goal
If you picked Hypertrophy, we don't force you onto a cut. Track changes are optional and last resort. The app serves your goal, not ours.
Offline First
Gyms have no signal. Job sites have no signal. Every workout must work without a connection. Nothing blocks on the network.
