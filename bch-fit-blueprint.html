03 / Training System

Six Tracks.
One Forever System.

All 6 tracks fully designed using a 15-week cycle model (3 four-week phases + deloads). Pick your goal, equipment, and days. App fills slots from the exercise library. Two foundational layers wrap every track: Recovery & Adaptation Engine (prevents overtraining) and Session-Length Scaling (30/45/60 min). 3–4 years before a single repeat.

01
Hard
to Kill
The generalist. Push/pull/legs strength + full-body conditioning every session. Weekly Undulating Periodization (rep ranges shift weekly across 3 blocks). App-generated conditioning from a curated movement pool: EMOM, For Time, Circuit, AMRAP formats, 10–16 min, always full body. The guy leaves every session wrecked in a different way.
For the guy who wants to not fall apart
02
Strength
Move heavy weight. Compound lifts, progressive overload, low rep ranges. For the guy with a rack and a number he wants to hit.
For the guy who wants bigger numbers
03
Hypertrophy
Build muscle. Look the part. Volume-focused. Nutrition is tightest here — the app enforces surplus and adjusts automatically.
For the guy who wants to look jacked
04
Performance
Athletic PPL: every session opens with a CNS-fresh explosive movement before the heavy compound. CNS-tiered opener volumes (Low/Mid/High/Dynamic Effort from Westside). 3 blocks: Strength-Speed, Power/Explosive, Work Capacity/Athletic. Block C includes skill-gated Olympic lifts (Clean & Jerk, full Snatch). 8–12 min conditioning finisher biases sprints/sled/plyo.
For the guy who wants to feel athletic
05
The Reset
The on-ramp. 8 weeks, full body 3x/week. Starts with dumbbells and machines, introduces barbells at week 5. Conditioning ramps from 5 to 14 minutes. Linear progression. Auto-prompts graduation into any track when complete.
For the guy who needs a starting line
06
No Gym
Real calisthenics with ladder-level progression (push 10 lvl, pull 11 lvl, squat 10 lvl). PPL split with skill work (handstands, levers, muscle-up progression). Running as primary conditioning (200m to mile-tempo). Muscle-building strategies baked in: tempo work, 100-rep sessions, weighted calisthenics via backpack, pyramid/drop-set regression ladders. 3 blocks: Foundation, Unilateral/Explosive, Density/Engine (Block C skill-gated).
For the guy with no excuses left
04 / Nutrition System

90% of
the Results.

The training is the scaffolding. The nutrition system is the building. Three phases, three nutrition styles, a meal library that learns, and food exclusions that respect how the guy actually lives. Built on Jaden's proprietary formulas and validated against his own numbers.

Pick Your Style

During onboarding, one question gates the entire nutrition UX: "How do you want to handle food?" This controls which features are visible, not how data is collected. Everyone logs every meal. Everyone tracks all 4 macro numbers. The style just determines what the app shows you.

STYLE
All In
"I'll meal prep and track everything." Gets grocery lists auto-generated from the week's meals, meal templates, saved cooler days. Suggestions bias toward prep-focused, home-cooked meals.
STYLE
Flexible
"I prep some, eat out some." Gets grocery lists only from meals he explicitly chose. Mix of prep meals, restaurant orders, and grab-and-go options in suggestions.
STYLE
No Prep
"Just tell me what to eat and what to avoid." Grocery lists and meal templates are hidden. Suggestions lean on restaurant orders, fast food smart orders, gas station combos, and the swap library. This guy needs the app the most.

Three Phases

1
Baseline Reset · 14 Days
Clean eating framework. No macros yet. Focus on WHAT, not HOW MUCH. Full food logging for ALL styles (barcode scan, search, or library pick). The 14-day clean streak is required to advance. Break it, counter resets. Streak intelligence checks every food log against the rules and flags violations before they cost you the streak.
2
Macro Optimization · Unlocked
Jaden's proprietary formula generates personalized macro targets. Track-aware (Hypertrophy forces surplus, Hard to Kill runs maintenance). Protein floor 150g, ceiling 250g. Calorie floor 1,800, ceiling 4,500. Full barcode scanning, meal templates, favorites, custom meals. Smart features activate: meal suggestions, deficit/surplus alerts, pre-workout timing.
3
Ongoing Adjustment · 16 Rules
16-rule adjustment engine. Daily emoji check-in (hunger/energy/training). Weight trending, macros recalculate automatically. Cardio before cuts: calorie drops are the LAST lever, not the first. The Prime Directive: guys feel BETTER, not worse. Inaction is the default; the app resists tinkering when signals are neutral.

Meal Library, Not Meal Plans

No prescribed meal plans. A browsable library of hundreds of meals with macros attached. The guy picks what he wants to eat, the app logs the macros and learns from his choices. Sources: Jaden's meal prep restaurant recipes (premium content no other app has), recipe data scrapes, swap list content already written (cooler meals, sandwich builds, fast food orders, gas station combos), and community submissions post-launch.

HOW IT WORKS
Browse or Get Suggestions
Guy browses the library or gets smart suggestions based on remaining macros. Picks what he wants, app logs it, shows remaining daily targets. Every meal tagged: macros, ingredients, exclusion flags, meal type, prep required, scenario (cooler/restaurant/gas station/home).
HOW IT WORKS
Zero-Cost Learning
The app gets smarter without a single API call. Frequency counters track picks. Favorites pin meals to the top. Time-of-day mapping (eggs in morning, dinner foods at night). Day-of-week patterns (Sunday = prep, Friday = restaurant). Rejection tracking drops skipped suggestions. All local, all free.

Food Exclusions

Framed as preferences, NOT allergies. This is a preference filter, not a medical device. User selects exclusions (dairy, eggs, shellfish, nuts, gluten, soy, fish, pork, red meat) and they disappear from the library and suggestions entirely. Optional dislikes (mushrooms, avocado, onions, etc.) get deprioritized but not hidden. Legal disclaimer required during onboarding. Changeable anytime in settings.

05 / Locked Principles

The Rules That
Don't Flex.

Every decision in the app traces back to one of these. If a feature can't justify itself against these principles, it doesn't ship.

Protein First
Every meal, swap, and recommendation leads with protein. No grilled cheese — there's no protein play.
Older Brother Voice
No "studies show." No "research says." Experience-based, direct, no hedging. This isn't a doctor. It's the older brother who's been there.
Feel Better, Not Worse
The Prime Directive of the adjustment engine. If a plan is tanking energy, mood, or training, the plan is wrong — not the user.
Cardio Before Cuts
Refeed → cardio → more cardio → THEN a tiny cut. Calorie drops are the last lever, not the first. Most apps get this exactly backwards.
Respect the Goal
If you picked Hypertrophy, we don't force you onto a cut. Track changes are optional and last resort. The app serves your goal, not ours.
Offline First
Gyms have no signal. Job sites have no signal. Every workout must work without a connection. Nothing blocks on the network.
06 / Smart Features

The Coach
In Your Pocket.

Rule-based intelligence built on Jaden's decision trees. Zero AI cost, zero API calls, zero marginal cost per user. Six learning mechanisms run on local data: frequency counters, favorites, time-of-day mapping, day-of-week patterns, rejection tracking, and exclusion filters. The AI Coach layers on top for the last 10%.

RULE-BASED
Smart Meal Suggestions
Calculates remaining macros, queries the meal library for matches within tolerance. Ranked by what the guy actually eats (frequency + favorites + time-of-day). Filtered by nutrition style: All In gets prep meals, Flexible gets a mix, No Prep gets restaurant orders and swap library picks. 3–5 options, always relevant.
RULE-BASED
Streak Intelligence
Every food entry checked against Phase 1 clean eating rules BEFORE logging. Flags violations and offers a better option so the guy can save his streak instead of losing it. Identifies weak spots ("you fall off on weekends") and adapts suggestions accordingly.
RULE-BASED
Smart Swaps
Scan something rough, app suggests a better alternative in the same category. Gas station: jerky and nuts, not the hot dog. Full swap library searchable by scenario: cooler meals, sandwich builds, restaurant orders, tired dinners, quick breakfasts.
RULE-BASED
Pre-Workout Timing
Knows your schedule. Nudges if no meal logged within 2–3 hours of training. Suggests food based on time until session and remaining macros.
RULE-BASED
Grocery List Generator
Generated FROM the meals the user chose for the week, organized by store section. Checkable shopping list UI. All In: auto-generated from full week's meals. Flexible: only from meals he explicitly picked. No Prep: feature is hidden (this guy doesn't cook). If no meals picked, no list appears.
PREMIUM
AI Coach (Claude)
Premium layer. Fed full user context: track, week, macros, training history, nutrition style, exclusions. Answers anything with YOUR numbers, not generic advice. The rule-based features handle 90% of the work so this layer costs pennies, not dollars.
