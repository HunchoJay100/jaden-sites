Walk-In Bath & Shower Programs
Dealer Comparison

One-day bath and shower remodels are a high-margin, high-demand category with no local installer between Sparta and Knoxville. This doc compares the five programs worth a conversation, ranked by fit with Flynn Development's existing infrastructure.

The Read

Best fit for Flynn Development is the wholesale supplier route (BCI Acrylic or Luxury Bath). You already have the crews, the local brand, and the marketing engine. A franchise pays for infrastructure you already own and gives up margin forever for brand recognition that compounds slower than your own name in this market.

The only reason to consider Jacuzzi, Kohler, or Re-Bath is if you want a turnkey sales process and are willing to trade 8-15% of revenue to not build it yourself.

The Three Business Models

Lowest Commitment

Wholesale / Independent Dealer

You buy product at wholesale from the manufacturer, install under your own brand, keep all margin, no royalty. BCI Acrylic, Luxury Bath, Bestbath.

Middle Ground

Licensed / Authorized Dealer

You sell a national brand (Jacuzzi, Kohler) in a protected territory. Moderate upfront fees, product purchase minimums, marketing co-op. Not a full franchise.

Highest Commitment

Franchise

Full brand system. Franchise fee upfront, ongoing royalty (typically 5-7%), national ad fund (typically 1-3%), required showroom, locked-in operating standards. Re-Bath, Bath Fitter.

BCI Acrylic
Wholesale Manufacturer
Model Wholesale. You are an independent dealer, not a franchisee. Install under Flynn Development brand.
Upfront Cost Low. Typical entry is dealer setup kit, samples, sales materials. No franchise fee. No territory buy-in.
Ongoing Fees None. Zero royalty. Zero ad fund. Margin on every job is 100% yours.
Territory Typically non-exclusive but lightly-protected. Confirm Sparta/Cookeville/Crossville coverage.
Product Scope Tub-to-shower conversions, replacement tubs, walk-in tubs, wall surrounds, shower bases. Full acrylic system.
Lead Time Custom pieces typically ship 2-4 weeks from order. Install itself is 1-2 days.

Why it fits Flynn Dev

  • You keep 100% of margin on every job forever
  • Install under Flynn Development name, strengthens local brand
  • No minimum volume lock-in if you stop selling it
  • Your crews already do this type of work
  • Stackable with current cost-plus and remodel lines

What you give up

  • No national TV ads driving leads to you
  • You build the brand recognition yourself
  • Limited sales training compared to franchise
  • You own marketing creative and lead gen entirely
Luxury Bath
Wholesale / Dealer Hybrid
Model Dealer network with wholesale pricing. Some dealers use Luxury Bath brand, others co-brand or use their own name.
Upfront Cost Low to moderate. Onboarding kit plus optional branded materials if you want to use their name.
Ongoing Fees No royalty. Optional marketing co-op if you use their brand.
Territory Dealer-by-dealer. Confirm whether the Sparta retailer is already a Luxury Bath dealer before committing.
Product Scope Full line: tub-to-shower, walk-ins, replacement, wall systems.
Lead Time Similar to BCI. Custom pieces 2-4 weeks typical.

Why to consider

  • Backup option if BCI can't serve the territory
  • Option to use their brand where it helps, your brand where it doesn't
  • No royalty commitment

What to watch

  • Weaker brand recognition than Jacuzzi or Kohler
  • Verify Sparta retailer isn't already their dealer
Jacuzzi Bath Remodel
Licensed Dealer
Model Licensed dealer program, not a full franchise. You sell Jacuzzi-branded bath remodel products in a protected territory.
Upfront Cost Moderate. Territory license fee, branded showroom or display requirements, sales training. Typically five-figure entry, not six.
Ongoing Fees Product purchase minimums. Marketing co-op contribution typical. No royalty on revenue in most structures, but verify.
Territory Exclusive or semi-exclusive territory. Given the Knoxville gap, Upper Cumberland likely available.
Product Scope Full bath remodel: tub-to-shower, walk-in tubs, replacement, wall systems. Jacuzzi-branded.
Lead Time Similar to BCI. 2-4 weeks custom, 1-2 day install.

Why to consider

  • Jacuzzi is the strongest consumer brand in the category
  • National ad spend drives brand-searched leads
  • Proven sales process and training
  • Territory protection if you commit

What you give up

  • Can only sell Jacuzzi-branded product
  • Brand equity builds for Jacuzzi, not Flynn Development
  • Product pricing premium vs. BCI for similar acrylic
  • Volume expectations once you sign
Kohler LuxStone / Walk-In Bath
Authorized Dealer
Model Authorized dealer of Kohler's bath remodel line. You must typically be an existing remodeler in good standing to qualify. Flynn Development qualifies.
Upfront Cost Moderate. Dealer onboarding, product samples, Kohler-branded showroom display. Lower entry than Jacuzzi.
Ongoing Fees Product purchase minimums. No royalty on revenue typical. Marketing MDF (market development funds) may be available.
Territory Less exclusive than Jacuzzi. Multiple Kohler dealers can coexist in a region.
Product Scope LuxStone walls (premium stone-composite), walk-in baths, showers, tub replacements. Highest-end product of the group.
Lead Time Custom pieces 3-5 weeks typical. Install 1-2 days.

Why to consider

  • Kohler is the most trusted premium brand in plumbing fixtures
  • LuxStone is genuinely higher-end than acrylic competitors
  • Pairs well with Flynn Dev's custom home positioning
  • No royalty structure typical

What to watch

  • Premium pricing limits market to higher-income buyers
  • Non-exclusive territory means future competition
  • Product purchase minimums are the real commitment
Re-Bath
Full Franchise
Model Full franchise. Operates under Re-Bath brand only. Required showroom, required operating standards, territory-based.
Upfront Cost High. Franchise fee plus required showroom buildout. Total investment typically runs into six figures per the FDD.
Ongoing Fees Royalty on revenue typical for franchises in this category (5-7% range). National ad fund contribution (1-3% range). Verify exact figures in FDD.
Territory Exclusive protected territory. Typically defined by population count or ZIP boundaries.
Product Scope Full bath remodel including complete bathrooms (not just bath and shower). Broadest scope of any program.
Lead Time Full bath remodels typically 2-5 days install, not one day. Custom order lead similar.

Why to consider

  • Most complete turnkey system
  • Broadest scope (full bath, not just tub/shower)
  • Strong exclusive territory protection
  • Full operating playbook provided

Why it likely isn't for Flynn Dev

  • Royalty forever on revenue you are generating with your own crews
  • Brand equity builds for Re-Bath, not you
  • Required showroom adds overhead you don't need
  • Highest commitment in the group for services you already perform
  • Franchise obligations constrain how you run the division
Note on specific numbers: royalty percentages, franchise fees, and territory minimums change year to year and vary by region. Any franchise (Re-Bath, Bath Fitter) must provide a Franchise Disclosure Document (FDD) that discloses exact figures. Dealer programs (BCI, Luxury Bath, Jacuzzi, Kohler) will quote current terms on a discovery call. Treat the ranges above as orientation, confirm specifics before committing.

Due Diligence Checklist

  1. Exact fee structure. Upfront, ongoing royalty, ad fund, product purchase minimums, marketing co-op, showroom requirements. Get it in writing.
  2. Territory definition. Exclusive or non-exclusive. If exclusive, exact ZIP or county boundaries. Confirm Cookeville, Sparta, Crossville, Livingston, Jamestown, Monterey all covered.
  3. Is Sparta retailer already their dealer? If yes, confirm whether they'd block you or if a second dealer is allowed. This is the single most important question on the Luxury Bath call.
  4. Lead flow. Do they send you leads from national advertising? What is the cost per lead, and are leads exclusive to you or shared with competing dealers?
  5. Sales training and closing ratio. What is the expected close rate on in-home estimates? Jacuzzi and Re-Bath publish ratios; wholesale programs don't.
  6. Exit terms. If you stop selling, what do you owe? Franchises have long tails. Wholesale is clean.
  7. Product lead time under current supply chain. Real lead time in 2026, not brochure lead time.
  8. Financing partners. Do they have approved consumer financing (GreenSky, Synchrony, etc.) built in? This is the close-rate multiplier on higher-ticket jobs.
  9. Warranty structure. Who eats a product defect callback, you or the manufacturer? How are labor claims handled?
  10. Reference dealers. Ask for 2-3 current dealers in similar markets (rural Tennessee, Upper South). Call them. Ask what they'd do differently.
