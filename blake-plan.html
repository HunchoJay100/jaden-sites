Hit these numbers, let the weight drop fast the first 4 weeks, and as your body wakes up we'll ramp the food up. By block three you'll be eating more than you are now and still dropping. § 04

Cardio

Locked From Day One
30MIN × 4 / WK

Fasted Walking

First thing in the morning, before breakfast. Steady pace — you should be able to hold a conversation but not sing a song.

This isn't punishment. This is the lever we pull before we ever touch your food again. If the scale stalls, we add minutes and days to cardio first. Calories are the last thing we cut.

§ 05

Check-In Protocol

Saturday · Weekly
SAT
EVERY WEEK

Weight + Spreadsheet

Log everything daily in a spreadsheet of your own — you know the drill. Every Saturday, send me your weight for the week and ship over the sheet. I'll review it, look for patterns, and send back any tweaks for the next 7 days. No guessing, no drifting — the data tells us what to do.

What to Track Daily
Weight (AM)
Same scale, same time, after bathroom, before food or water.
Sleep + CPAP
Hours slept and whether you wore the CPAP. Recovery is a lift.
Energy 1–5
Gut check on how you feel. Crashing energy = we adjust.
Hunger 1–5
Two days of 4–5 hunger = metabolism is asking for food. We'll give it.
Training Session
Lift, sets, reps, weight. Note AMRAP rep counts — those drive the next cycle.
Cardio Minutes
Fasted walk duration. Four sessions banked = a clean week.
Calories
Hit the target or flag where you landed. Be honest — the math doesn't care.
Macros (P / C / F)
225 / 175 / 70. Protein first, always.
Notes
Sore spots, off days, travel, anything weird. Pattern spotting starts here.
