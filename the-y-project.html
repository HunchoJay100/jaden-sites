Why We Exist

Everyone has a why.
Most people never chase it.

The Y Project exists to change that. We're a community of people who believe that purpose isn't something you find. It's something you build. Through action, through service, and through showing up for the people who need it most.

We crowdfund for charities and organizations that are doing real work on the ground. We rally around kids and individuals with special needs. And we hold each other accountable to living lives that actually mean something.

Purpose-Driven Community

Connect with people who are chasing something real. Not followers. Not clout. Purpose.

Crowdfunding for Good

We pool resources and direct them toward charities, organizations, and causes that create tangible impact.

Kids & Special Needs

Our core focus. Every child deserves opportunity. Every person with special needs deserves advocacy and support.

Living Your Why
