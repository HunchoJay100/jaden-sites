# THE FOOD PROTOCOL: QUICK START (WOMEN)

*Screenshot this. Live by it for 42 days.*

---

## YOUR DAILY MACROS

**Step 1.** Pick your **desired weight**. The weight you're trying to get to (15-30 lb below where you are today is a good starting point if you don't have a clear goal).

**Step 2.** Run the formula:

- **Protein** = your desired weight × 0.8g _(max **140g**)_
- **Carbs** = **150g**
- **Fat** = **60g**

**Step 3.** Save your three numbers (screenshot, notes app, lock screen, whatever). You won't do this math again.

---

## YOUR DAILY ACTION

1. Eat **3-5 meals** built from the food list (in the full guide).
2. **Track every meal** in Claude, ChatGPT, or MyFitnessPal.
3. **Stay at or under** your daily totals.
4. **Post your check-in** in the group.

Repeat for 42 days.

> **First 10-14 days, expect to fall short on macros.** That's fine. Eating clean food is more volume than you're used to. Quality is non-negotiable. Quantity catches up. Only eat from the approved foods, even if you can't hit your numbers yet.

---

## SET UP YOUR AI TRACKER IN 60 SECONDS

Open Claude or ChatGPT, tap the mic, and read this aloud (fill in your numbers):

> "You are my fitness tracker for the next 6 weeks. I'm doing a 42-day fat-loss challenge. My daily macro targets are [your protein]g protein, [your carbs]g carbs, and [your fat]g fat. Every time I tell you what I ate, estimate the macros for that meal and keep a running total for the day. When I ask, tell me how close I am to my targets and what I have left. If I'm short on protein with one meal to go, suggest a quick fix. Don't lecture me about food choices. Just track and tally. Reset the running total each morning. Ready?"

After that, just tell it what you eat as you eat it. Use voice. It takes 10 seconds per meal.

---

## THE NON-NEGOTIABLES

- **Two drinks per week max.** A ceiling, not a goal. Zero is better if you can hold it.
- **Track every meal.** No exceptions.
- **Don't bust your daily totals.** Going under is fine, especially the first two weeks. Going over breaks the protocol.

---

## IF YOU GET STUCK

**Bring it to the group first.** Jaden and Amber are active in the group every day. If you're hungry, too full, can't find a food on the list, eating somewhere weird, traveling, or just stuck, **post it**. Do not deviate from the plan on your own. We'll find a solution together. That's the whole point of doing this with a group instead of a PDF.

- **Don't know what to eat?** Open the full guide, Build Your Plate. Still stuck? Drop the question in the group.
- **Anything else?** Post in the group. Don't guess. Don't quit. Ask us first.
