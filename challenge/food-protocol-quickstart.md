## YOUR DAILY MACROS

**Step 1 —** Weigh yourself this morning, after the bathroom, before food or coffee.

**Step 2 —** Use the formula for your gender:

### WOMEN
- **Protein** = your bodyweight × 0.8g _(max **140g**)_
- **Carbs** = **150g**
- **Fat** = **60g**

### MEN
- **Protein** = your bodyweight × 1.0g _(max **220g**)_
- **Carbs** = **220g**
- **Fat** = **65g**

**Step 3 —** Write your three numbers below. Done. You won't do this math again.

> **My daily macros:**
> Protein: _______g
> Carbs:   _______g
> Fat:     _______g

---

## YOUR DAILY ACTION

1. Eat **3-4 meals** built from the food list (in the full guide).
2. **Track every meal** in Claude, ChatGPT, or MyFitnessPal.
3. **Hit your macros** within ~10g of target.
4. **Post your check-in** in the group.

Repeat for 42 days.

---

## THE NON-NEGOTIABLES

- **No alcohol.** None. Zero. 42 days.
- **Track every meal.** No exceptions.
- **Hit your macros.** Within ~10g.

---

## IF YOU GET STUCK

- **Hungry past day 4?** Add 30g of carbs to your daily target. Still hungry 3-4 days later? Add 30g more. Max two adjustments.
- **Don't know what to eat?** Open the full guide → Build Your Plate.
- **Eating out / gas station?** Open the full guide → When Real Life Happens.
- **Anything else?** Post in the group. Someone has been there.
