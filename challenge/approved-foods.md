# THE APPROVED FOODS

---

Build every meal of the next 42 days from these foods.

If a food is not on this list, it is not on the protocol.

For your recommended serving sizes, check your Full Guide (Men or Women).

---

## 1. Protein

- Chicken breast
- Lean beef (90/10)
- Sirloin
- Filet
- Ribeye
- New York strip
- Ground turkey
- White fish (cod, tilapia, mahi)
- Salmon
- Eggs
- Egg whites
- Shrimp
- Deli meat (Boar's Head turkey, roast beef, ham, or chicken)
- Greek yogurt (2% plain, Fage-style)
- Cottage cheese (low-fat)
- Whey protein
- Tuna (canned)

---

## 2. Carbs

- Baked potato
- Red potato
- Hash browns

- Sourdough

- Banana
- Berries
- Apple

- Pineapple
- Corn tortilla
- Flour tortilla

---

## 3. Fat

- Olive oil
- Butter or ghee
- Coconut oil
- Avocado
- Almonds
- Cashews
- Walnuts
- Pecans
- Natural peanut butter
- Natural almond butter
- Shredded cheddar cheese
- Grated parmesan
- Whole eggs
- Dark chocolate (70%+)

---

**Vegetables are a free-for-all.** They don't count against your macros for our purposes. Eat them whenever you want. They fill you up between meals.
