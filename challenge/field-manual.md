## The Idea

The protocol works at your kitchen table. But you don't live at your kitchen table.

You eat out. You travel. You forget to prep. You end up at a gas station with no clean food in sight. This is what to do when you're not at home but still on the protocol.

Two situations covered. Two answers. Same protein-first spine that runs the rest of the protocol.

## 1. Eating Out

You're not cooking. Someone else is. The rule changes: pick a clean order, ignore the temptations, drink water, eat slow.

**A note on portion sizing.** Men lean toward the larger end of these orders (double protein at Chipotle, 6-8 oz steak, 12-count nuggets at Chick-fil-A). Women lean toward the smaller end (single protein, 4-6 oz steak, 8-count nuggets). Your macros are different. Your plate should be too.

### Chipotle (the gold standard)
- **Order:** Chicken or steak burrito bowl, white or brown rice, black beans, fajita veggies, pico, salsa, romaine
- **Pick one fat:** guac, cheese, or sour cream. Not all three.
- **Men:** double protein. **Women:** single protein.
- **Skip:** Chips, queso, flour tortilla, sweet tea

### Chick-fil-A
- **Order:** Grilled Nuggets (12-count for men, 8-count for women) + Market Salad (light avocado lime dressing), or Grilled Chicken Sandwich (no mayo)
- **Drink:** Water or unsweet tea
- **Skip:** Waffle fries, regular nuggets, CFA sauce, lemonade

### Panera
- **Order:** Power chicken hummus bowl, Mediterranean bowl with chicken, or any Power Salad with chicken
- **Skip:** Sandwiches (processed bread), pastries, mac and cheese, frozen lemonades

### Subway / Jersey Mike's / Jimmy John's / Firehouse
- **Order:** Any sub "in a tub" or unwich or bowl. Lettuce base, no bread. Real meat (turkey, roast beef, tuna, chicken). Load the veggies. Oil and vinegar.
- **Skip:** Fake steak, teriyaki sauce, chips, cookie

### Any Steakhouse (Texas Roadhouse, Outback, Longhorn, Logan's, etc.)
- **Order:** Sirloin + plain baked or sweet potato + broccoli or green beans + side salad with vinaigrette
- **Men:** 6-8 oz sirloin. **Women:** 4-6 oz sirloin.
- **The trick:** Tell them not to bring the bread basket. That is the move.
- **Skip:** Bloomin' Onion, loaded baked potato, croutons

### Olive Garden
- **Order:** Grilled chicken + steamed broccoli + 1 cup pasta if you have carb room
- **Skip:** Breadsticks (this is the trap), creamy pastas, fried apps

### Wendy's
- **Order:** Grilled Chicken Sandwich (no mayo, no cheese), or Apple Pecan Salad with grilled chicken, or chili + plain baked potato
- **Skip:** Fries, Frosty, baked potato with toppings

### Buffalo Wild Wings
- **Order:** Naked (unbreaded) wings, traditional, dry rub or low-sugar sauce. Veggies or plain fries on the side.
- **Skip:** Anything breaded, mango habanero, honey BBQ

### Hibachi / Sushi
- **Hibachi:** Chicken or steak + veggies + light rice. Skip the sauce baths.
- **Sushi:** Sashimi and nigiri. Skip deep-fried rolls and anything with cream cheese.

### Mexican Sit-Down
- **Order:** Chicken, steak, or shrimp fajitas + rice + beans + pico + guac
- **Skip:** Flour tortillas (go corn), chimichangas, cheese-sauce-covered anything

### Pizza Night (family life is real)
- 2 slices real pizza (thin crust, real cheese, real toppings) + a big salad with grilled chicken
- Better than skipping family dinner. Quality-matched trade.

### McDonald's (last resort)
- **Breakfast:** Egg McMuffin (egg + Canadian bacon + cheese + English muffin, real-ish food)
- **Lunch:** Grilled Chicken Sandwich (no mayo, no cheese)
- **Skip:** Fries, soda, McGriddles, frappes, hash browns

## 2. Gas Station Survival

The formula: **Protein shake + banana + jerky + handful of nuts = complete meal, under $10, five-minute stop.** Every guy in America is within ten miles of this.

### Protein
- Jerky or biltong (short ingredient list, low sugar)
- Hard-boiled eggs (single-pack)
- String cheese
- Rolled deli meat pack (eat like jerky)
- Core Power or Fairlife protein shake
- Protein bars (Quest, Built, David, Barebells, RXBAR. Check macros.)
- Starbucks egg bites
- Chomps chicken sausage stick

### Carb / Fruit
- Bananas, apples, oranges
- Plain Greek yogurt cup (Fage 2%, Chobani Less Sugar)
- Plain rice cakes
- Microwave rice pouch

### Fat
- Mixed nuts (dry roasted, salted, not honey roasted)
- Avocado (some stations carry them)
- 1 oz dark chocolate (70%+)

**Avoid anywhere:** anything in cellophane that crunches, anything "energy" or "smoothie", anything with corn syrup in the first three ingredients.
