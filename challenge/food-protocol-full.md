## How to Eat for the Next 42 Days

## The Idea

We're not giving you a meal plan.

Meal plans work for 6 weeks and then you're back where you started, because nobody taught you how to eat. They handed you a list of someone else's meals to follow, and the day you ran out of recipes, you ran out of the program.

This doc teaches you the system. Three macros. A list of foods. A way to build a plate. A tracker on your phone. Run it for 42 days and you'll know how to eat for the rest of your life.

Three rules underneath everything:

1. **Hit your macros** — every day.
2. **Track every meal** — no exceptions.
3. **No alcohol** — full 42 days.

The math will work. The discipline is on you.

## Step 1 — Calculate Your Daily Macros

Weigh yourself first thing in the morning, after the bathroom, before food or coffee. Use that number.

### Women
- **Protein:** bodyweight × 0.8g — capped at **140g**
- **Carbs:** **150g** flat
- **Fat:** **60g** flat

### Men
- **Protein:** bodyweight × 1.0g — capped at **220g**
- **Carbs:** **220g** flat
- **Fat:** **65g** flat

Write these three numbers down. Put them on the fridge, the lock screen, the bathroom mirror — somewhere you'll see them. You don't need to recalculate every week.

### Why these numbers

**Protein scales with bodyweight** because it's doing the heavy work — preserving the muscle you have while you lose fat. Lose protein, lose muscle, tank your metabolism. That's the trap most diets fall into. Eating enough protein is what makes the difference between *losing weight* and *changing your body composition*.

**Carbs and fat stay flat** because hunger and energy needs don't scale linearly with bodyweight. A 200lb woman doesn't need proportionally more food than a 130lb woman to function — especially after years of yo-yo dieting that suppresses metabolism. Flat targets put everyone in a real deficit without leaving anyone crushed.

**The protein caps exist** because past 140g (women) / 220g (men), more protein doesn't add anything for most people, and it's genuinely hard to eat that much meat day after day. Caps make the protocol *executable*.

## Step 2 — Set Up Tracking (5 minutes)

You're going to track every meal for 42 days. Pick one tool, set it up today.

### Option A: Claude or ChatGPT (easiest, voice-first)

This is what we use. Total setup time: zero.

1. Open Claude or ChatGPT on your phone.
2. Tap the microphone.
3. After every meal, say what you ate: *"I just ate 4 eggs, a cup of oatmeal, and 2 tablespoons of peanut butter. Give me the macros and add it to my running total for today."*
4. The AI tells you the macros and keeps your daily total.
5. End of day, ask: *"How am I doing on my targets — 116 protein, 150 carbs, 60 fat?"*

That's it. No food database, no barcode scanner, no logging app. You talk to your phone, your phone tells you the math.

### Option B: MyFitnessPal (for the spreadsheet types)

If you want it more structured:

1. Download MyFitnessPal (the free version is fine).
2. In settings, set custom macro goals — enter your protein, carbs, and fat targets.
3. Log every meal. Use the barcode scanner on packaged stuff.
4. End of day — check the dashboard.

Both work. Pick the one you'll actually use.

The "I don't know how to track" excuse died the day voice mode launched. You can tell your phone what you ate while you walk to your car.

## Step 4 — How to Build Your Plate

Every meal is the same shape:

> **1 PROTEIN + 1 CARB + 1 FAT**

That's it. Pull each from the lists above. Build 3-4 meals a day. Track everything.

### Per meal, aim for:

- **Protein:** 4-6 oz cooked meat or fish, 1 cup yogurt or cottage cheese, or a scoop of whey + something else
- **Carb:** ½ to 1 cup cooked rice / pasta / oats, 1 sweet potato, or 1 piece of fruit
- **Fat:** 1 tbsp oil or butter, ¼ avocado, 1 oz nuts, or 2 tbsp nut butter

### Rules of thumb

- **Protein anchors the meal.** Pick it first, build everything else around it.
- **Carbs go bigger at breakfast and lunch, smaller at dinner.** This is when you need them — to fuel your workout, your work, and your walk.
- **Fat usually rides along** with the protein (eggs, salmon, steak) or shows up as a finisher (oil on rice, butter on potato). You don't need to add fat on purpose at every meal — let the protein do most of the work.
- **If a meal feels too small, add another carb serving.** Not another protein. Protein is sized to your daily target; you don't need more at any one meal.
- **Vegetables are a free-for-all.** They don't count against your macros for our purposes. Eat them whenever you want — they fill you up between meals.

The samples below show what this looks like in practice. Yours will look different — different proteins, different carbs, your favorites. The shape stays the same.

## The Hunger Rule

You're going to be hungry sometimes. That's fat loss working. But there's hungry, and there's *starving*.

If you're starving past **day 4**:
- Add **30g of carbs** to your daily target. About ¾ cup more rice, ¾ more sweet potato, or a banana plus some berries.

Still starving **3-4 days after that**?
- Add **another 30g of carbs.** Max two adjustments — that's it.

The goal is fat loss you can sustain for **42 days**, not 4 days of suffering followed by a binge. If you adjust, you're not failing. You're calibrating.

## What Doesn't Count

- **Alcohol.** Zero. None. The protocol breaks the day you drink. This rule alone changes your sleep, your skin, your mood, and your weight before the workouts even kick in.
- **Liquid calories** (soda, juice, sweet tea, sweetened coffee drinks). Track them if you have them, but they're empty calories that don't fill you up. Better to spend those macros on real food.
- **"It's just a bite."** Track it. The whole point is learning what you actually eat. The bites add up. Pretending they don't is how you finish 42 days with nothing to show for it.

## Your Daily Routine

Five minutes of admin a day. The rest is just eating, training, and living.

1. **Weigh yourself in the morning** (3x/week minimum, daily if you can stomach it — write it down).
2. **Plan tonight's meals roughly the night before** — what's defrosting, what's on the stove.
3. **Track every meal** in your app of choice.
4. **End of day check:** did I hit my macros? Yes or no.
5. **Post your check-in to the group.** ✅ on macros + workout + walk + reading + no alcohol.
