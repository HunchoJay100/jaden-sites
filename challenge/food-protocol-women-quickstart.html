  1. Save your three numbers (screenshot, notes app, lock screen, whatever). You won't do this math again.

Your Daily Action

  1. Eat 3-5 meals built from the food list (in the full guide).
  2. Track every meal in Claude, ChatGPT, or MyFitnessPal.
  3. Stay at or under your daily totals.
  4. Post your check-in in the group.
Repeat for 42 Days
First 10-14 Days

Expect to fall short on macros. Clean food is more volume than you're used to. Your appetite catches up as your body adapts. Only eat from the approved foods. Quality is non-negotiable. Quantity catches up.

Set Up Your AI Tracker

Open Claude or ChatGPT on your phone, tap the mic, and read this aloud. Fill in your three numbers where you see the highlighted spots.

"You are my fitness tracker for the next 6 weeks. I'm doing a 42-day fat-loss challenge. My daily macro targets are [protein]g protein, [carbs]g carbs, and [fat]g fat. Every time I tell you what I ate, estimate the macros for that meal and keep a running total for the day. When I ask, tell me how close I am to my targets and what I have left. If I'm short on protein with one meal to go, suggest a quick fix. Don't lecture me about food choices. Just track and tally. Reset the running total each morning. Ready?"

After that, just say what you ate as you eat it. 10 seconds per meal.

The Non-Negotiables

Two drinks per week max. Ceiling, not a goal.
Track every meal. No exceptions.
Don't bust your daily totals. Going under is fine, especially the first two weeks. Going over breaks the protocol.
